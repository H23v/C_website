EXON is a multiplayer game that immerses players in a techno-syncretic cult where technology is transcendentally worshipped as a deity. The cult is committed to replicating the natural realm with the goal of saving the world from ecosystem instability. Discover the truth behind the cult on the Roblox platform.

Within the project I worked on the narrative construction of the game and on the development of its visual communication. My role focused on shaping the tone and conceptual atmosphere of EXON, then translating that identity into posters, web materials, workflow documentation and exhibition-facing assets capable of presenting the project consistently across different contexts.

2025 © The video game was designed as part of the Virtual Realities and Paradigms of Complexity course at the Brera Academy of Fine Arts in Milan, starting from feelings of curiosity about creating online communities.
